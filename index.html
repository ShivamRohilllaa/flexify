About the practice

Meet Dr. Neeraj — a physiotherapist who listens first.

Patients describe Dr. Neeraj as "kind, supportive and easy to talk to". At Flexiphy, we don't rush appointments or hand you a printed sheet — we spend the time to understand your pain, your history and what a better week looks like for you.
